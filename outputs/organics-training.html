Compost

Organics

Give your food scraps a second life — don't send them to landfill. Organic waste in Australian landfills generates around 13 million tonnes of greenhouse gas emissions every year.

✓ Goes in

  • Fruit and vegetable scraps
  • Coffee grounds
  • Leftover food (no liquids)
  • Bones & eggshells

✕ Never goes in

  • Liquids — tip these down a sink first
  • Tea bags (the plastic mesh inside contaminates compost)
  • Soft plastic packaging
  • Biodegradable or compostable bags and takeaway containers
  • Cutlery, straws, or plastic-lined cups
💡Keep it simple: if it can be eaten, it can go in the organics bin.
