Batteries

A battery in the wrong bin isn't just a recycling mistake — it's a genuine fire risk. Damaged or crushed batteries can ignite inside a collection bin or a waste truck, and that fire is very hard to put out once it starts.

✓ Where it goes

  • Every battery — of any size — goes to your building's dedicated battery collection point
  • Not sure where that is? Ask your Waste Champion or Building Management

✕ Never goes in

  • General waste, Mixed Recycling, or any other stream
  • The E-Waste point — batteries need their own separate, dedicated point
💡A single battery in the wrong bin can start a fire in a waste truck — this is the one rule on this page that's about safety, not just sorting.
